# Ternary Phase Equilibria: CaO–Al₂O₃–SiO₂ Explorer

An interactive study tool for reading the CaO–Al₂O₃–SiO₂ ternary phase diagram (MSE 3001, Ch 14).

Open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the GitHub Pages link once Pages is enabled.

## What it does

- **Free explore:** click or drag a melt composition anywhere in the triangle.
  - Highlights its compatibility triangle and primary field.
  - Gives the final three solids and their amounts (tie-triangle math).
  - Shows the temperature where the last liquid disappears, and warns when the first solid will be resorbed.
- **Worked examples (P, Q, R):** animated cooling paths.
  - The liquid's composition moves across the diagram.
  - Tie lines and live phase-fraction bars follow along.
  - Covers a normal path to a eutectic, resorption at a ternary peritectic, and gradual resorption.
- **Layers:** Alkemade lines (solids that coexist), boundary curves (liquid with two solids), invariant points and primary fields.

## Notes on accuracy

- Compound positions are calculated exactly from stoichiometry, in wt%.
- Boundary curves, primary fields and invariant temperatures are approximate, traced from a published diagram as straight segments.
- Liquid paths in the examples are schematic. Isotherms are not drawn.

Use it to build intuition, not for precise readings.
